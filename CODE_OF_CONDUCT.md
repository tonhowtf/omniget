# Code of conduct

OmniGet follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep criticism about the work and not the person, and do not harass anyone. This applies to issues, pull requests, the translation project and the community chat.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour is a problem, write to [contato@getomniget.com](mailto:contato@getomniget.com). Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block accounts that break these rules, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.

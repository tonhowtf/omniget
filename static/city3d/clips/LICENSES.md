# Licenses of the animation clips

`clips.json` is generated by `src-tauri/omniget-avatar/tools/gen_clips.ts` from
the procedural clip definitions in `src/lib/city3d/avatar/clips.ts` (OmniGet,
GPL-3.0 code). The generated keyframes are dedicated to the public domain
(CC0 1.0).

The Quaternius Universal Animation Library 1 and 2 (CC0) were the planned
source, but they are only distributed through Patreon/itch download pages and
could not be fetched by the build. Retargeted Quaternius clips can replace or
extend these entries later with the same format (quaternions per VRM bone in
the normalized T-pose rig, hips offset in hips-height units).

The joint vocabulary (flex, extend, abduct, twist...) follows the idea of
posecode (posecode-dev/posecode, Apache-2.0 parser); no posecode code is used.

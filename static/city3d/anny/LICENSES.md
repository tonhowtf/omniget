# Licenses of the baked Anny body data

The files in this folder (`anny_lod1229.bin`, `anny_lod369.bin`, `manifest.json`)
are generated by `src-tauri/omniget-avatar/tools/bake_anny.py` from the Anny
parametric human body model.

- **Anny** (naver/anny): source code under the Apache License 2.0,
  Copyright 2025 NAVER Corp. Only the data is used here; the phenotype mixer
  (`_get_phenotype_blendshape_coefficients`) is re-implemented in TypeScript
  (`src/lib/city3d/avatar/anny.ts`) following its logic.
  https://github.com/naver/anny , https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
- **MakeHuman / MPFB2 assets** (base mesh, reduced topologies, phenotype
  targets, local modifiers, skeleton and skin weights), as redistributed by
  Anny in `data/mpfb2`: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).
  https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- **Face Units** by Mika Suominen (the 52 ARKit-named facial actions), as
  redistributed by Anny in `data/faceunits01`: CC0 1.0 Universal.

What was derived: the two reduced topologies (`notoes_collapse10pc`,
`notoes_collapse3pc`) re-skinned onto the 52-bone VRM humanoid skeleton, posed
in VRM T-pose, a PCA basis of the phenotype space (float16), the exact
phenotype-to-PCA table, 64 curated local face changes and the 52 facial
actions as sparse float16 deltas.

The clothing and hair of the city avatars are procedural geometry generated by
OmniGet at runtime (GPL-3.0 code, the generated shapes are dedicated CC0).
MakeHuman MHCLO garments (CC0, fitted by `base_mesh_vertex_indices`) are the
planned next step; every imported piece keeps its own license in the catalog.
